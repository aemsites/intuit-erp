## Company

Lango — an AI-enabled language services company based in Austin, Texas.
Scaled rapidly through the acquisition of seven businesses across the US.

By Bryan Bui · Published May 4, 2026

## Results at a glance

- **66%** — faster month-end close
- **90%** — reduction in aged accounts receivable (from 22% down to under 2%)
- **25–30%** — of the accounting team's weekly capacity recovered from manual spreadsheet reconciliation

## Narrative sections (in order)

1. **Transforming language services with AI** — Lango is a mission-driven,
   AI-enabled language services leader that scaled through 7 acquisitions
   across the US. That growth created a labyrinth of fragmented data that
   threatened to stall operations. Moving to Intuit Enterprise Suite
   replaced a manual reporting nightmare with a consolidated financial
   platform, slashing aged AR from 22% to under 2% and giving leadership
   the real-time visibility to manage board expectations and debt
   covenants.

2. **The risks of raising capital with manual data** — Each of the 7
   acquisitions brought its own QuickBooks instance, creating (in
   Daneshforooz's words) "a labyrinth of logins and a maze of manual
   mapping" in Excel. The team was losing 25–30% of their weekly capacity
   reconciling seven different spreadsheets, and month-end close often
   dragged on for 45 days. Pull quote: *"Without unified data, you can't
   manage cash, you can't manage growth, and you definitely can't manage
   trust."*

3. **Modern versus traditional ERP solutions** — The obvious next step
   looked like NetSuite, but it didn't hold up under CFO-level scrutiny:
   "NetSuite carried a hefty implementation fee and a recurring annual
   cost tens of thousands more than we were willing to spend," and the
   system felt "old-school, and clunky."

4. **Efficiency, velocity, and strategy at scale** — Lango chose Intuit
   Enterprise Suite for three reasons: ease of use, cost-efficiency, and a
   consolidated multi-entity architecture. New acquisitions could be
   "bolted on" by simply upgrading a QuickBooks Online account into the
   Intuit Enterprise Suite account, instead of requiring what Daneshforooz
   calls "a PhD to figure it out." Multi-entity Consolidation and Custom
   Financial Roles turned the back office from an Excel center into a
   strategic asset. Pull quote: *"In our industry, having access to data
   is critical. Intuit Enterprise Suite took us from a mess of seven
   different spreadsheets to a consolidated view that allows me to manage
   the business, communicate with my board, and manage debt partners with
   total confidence."*

5. **Simplicity at an enterprise level** — With the financial foundation
   stabilized, Lango's playbook is built on simplicity: targeting further
   acquisitions and using Intuit Enterprise Suite to fold them into
   consolidated financials instantly, with an aggressive goal of
   streamlining the finance team via automation and AI even as transaction
   volume scales — positioning the company for growth and a potential
   public offering down the road. Closing quote: *"I want a software
   platform that facilitates simplification. Intuit Enterprise Suite
   allows us to make everything as simple as possible, but not simpler."*
