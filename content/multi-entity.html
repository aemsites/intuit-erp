Frequently asked questions

Can you support entities outside the US?

Not at this time.

Can I still keep all of my data and users separate across companies?

Yes, you can set granular user permissions that restrict access by company.

What data can I share? Can I share user access across companies?

Intuit Enterprise Suite allows you to share aggregated or single-source views of financial and operational data across your entire group of companies. Admins can share access to consolidated data and specific multi-entity functions across companies, all while maintaining strong internal controls.
