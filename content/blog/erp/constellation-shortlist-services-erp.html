Intuit Enterprise Suite named to Constellation ShortList for services-centric cloud ERP

By Intuit team

Intuit Enterprise Suite is named to Constellation Research’s Shortlist for Services-Centric Cloud ERP for Q1 2026. Intuit Enterprise Suite was included in this shortlist because of its ability to deliver a critical transformation to organizations wanting to achieve efficiencies in the age of AI.

We’re thrilled to be included in Constellation Research’s shortlist. It’s an acknowledgment of our focus to deliver an AI-native ERP that unifies data, automates workflows, and delivers AI-driven insights that give visibility into performance to drive growth.

Ashley Still, EVP and GM, Mid-Market, at Intuit

The Constellation ShortList is designed to help organizations identify the technology solutions best suited to support their digital transformation initiatives. Each ShortList narrows a crowded market into a focused set of vendors worth serious consideration. The Services-Centric Cloud ERP Shortlist category recognizes companies that provide financial and professional services capabilities for organizations that deliver services to customers.
