Intuit Enterprise Suite shows off its AI-native ERP capabilities at FATE

By Intuit team

The Intuit Enterprise Suite team showed up in full force last week during the second annual Finance and Accounting Technology Expo (FATE), Nov. 13-14, at the famous Javits Convention Center in New York City. Touted as the “largest gathering of finance and accounting technology professionals in the country,” the two days were buzzing with more than 2000 CFOs, CPAs, accounting and tax firm partners, controllers, and other tech-forward financial leaders.

At FATE, the Intuit Enterprise Suite team demonstrated how the AI-native ERP is transforming the mid-market software industry. A more nimble solution than legacy ERPs, Intuit Enterprise Suite provides growing businesses with a configurable online platform that powers growth.

Demos, deep dives, and freestyle at the expo

Inside the dynamic, welcoming booth, the team provided comprehensive demos showcasing how Intuit Enterprise Suite meets the needs of modern CFOs. Visitors learned about AI-powered finance tools that help with faster decision making and allow for simpler multi-entity control.

One breakout session featured a deep dive into some of Intuit Enterprise Suite’s AI-native ERP capabilities. In another, Ryan Steck, CFO and partner, Ignite Spot, a US-based bookkeeping and accounting services firm, delivered a presentation on the evolution of AI in accounting. He focused on the important role AI plays in elevating the role of accountants as strategic advisors to their clients.

Finally, freestyle rapper Harry Mack brought a fresh perspective to finance and accounting on the Expo floor. Engaging attendees with improvisational techniques, Mack represented the transition from clunky, outdated, legacy ERP systems to Intuit Enterprise Suite. The modern system is a painless, powerful, and proven solution that’s easy to learn, quick to implement, and customizable to fit the unique needs of growing mid-market businesses.

Intuit Enterprise Suite is transforming the ERP market

Today’s growing mid-market businesses are complex. They’re often overserved by legacy ERP systems that are expensive, time-consuming, and costly to install and learn.

Mid-market businesses are also over-digitized, using up to 25 different apps to manage and run the business. This leads to a fragmented tech stack with data silos, resulting in limited visibility into business performance, a lack of integrated workflows, and an inability to make data-backed, confident decisions.

Intuit Enterprise Suite is transforming how more complex, mid-market businesses grow, eliminating the need for companies to adopt costly, legacy ERPs or juggle multiple applications to manage their operations. It provides businesses with an adaptable, intelligent, AI-native ERP solution that includes multi-entity and multi-dimensional financial management capabilities, business intelligence and reporting, payments and bill pay, project profitability, payroll, HR, and marketing, in one connected, scalable, cloud-based platform. It also provides tailored workflows and reports and connects to an extensive partner ecosystem to create an optimized and customized experience that meets unique, industry-specific needs.
