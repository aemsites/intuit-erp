About this research

The 2026 Intuit Enterprise Technology Benchmark Report is based on an Intuit-commissioned online survey conducted in November 2025, which gathered insights from 2,000 senior US business leaders and executives at companies with over $2.5 million in annual revenue. The robust sample focused on finance and technology leaders (53%) from mostly growing businesses (77%) and used a 41-question survey with statistical significance tested at a 95% confidence level to confidently highlight differences across business cohorts.
