Empowering the modern restaurant industry

FIXE is not just a bookkeeping firm; it’s a specialized growth partner for the restaurant industry founded by restaurateur-turned-CEO Ryan Handel. By combining financial services, the right technology, and a proprietary dashboard, FIXE’s mission is to enable restaurants to scale from single units to regional powerhouses of 60+ locations. With Intuit Enterprise Suite, FIXE delivers what Handel describes as “service with bookkeeping alongside best-in-class software,” leveraging the Suite’s multi-entity and consolidated reporting strengths so clients don’t need to “graduate” to expensive, disjointed ERP systems as they grow.

Restaurant growing pains expanding into mid-market

For years, FIXE faced a recurring vulnerability. As restaurant clients expanded to manage dozens of entities, they would inevitably hit the functional limits of QuickBooks Online, prompting high-value clients to evaluate solutions such as R365, Sage Intacct, or NetSuite in search of robust multi-entity management. Without a dedicated mid-market offering, Handel says this “graduation” represented a significant risk for FIXE: potential client churn and a disruptive “rip and replace” implementation for the restaurant group.

A strategic up-market shift with Intuit Enterprise Suite

Adopting Intuit Enterprise Suite fundamentally changed FIXE’s market position, providing the multi-entity consolidation and intercompany accounting FIXE needed without the heavy implementation costs, extended timelines, or steep learning curves of traditional ERPs. For one multi-state restaurant group that buys ingredients and distributes them to all its locations via a centralized commissary entity, Intuit Enterprise Suite automated a web of intercompany billing that was previously a manual nightmare.

Allowed us to service and sell to restaurant groups we simply couldn’t have handled before.

Ryan Handel, CEO, FIXE

Reclaiming time and generating revenue at scale

The return on investment for FIXE was immediate. Right away, Intuit Enterprise Suite helped FIXE land two large clients representing north of $35,000 a month, and Handel emphasizes it’s not only an operational tool, but a driver of new growth. By eliminating the “swivel chair” data entry previously required for complex groups, FIXE’s account managers each save at least 10 to 15 hours a month, and onboarding QuickBooks Online clients to Intuit Enterprise Suite now takes days instead of months. One client — a pizza chain operating 23 locations with approximately $50 million in annual revenue — now relies on Intuit Enterprise Suite to secure its financial infrastructure as it prepares for future franchising and potential public-market funding.

Supporting FIXE’s roadmap to 10,000 locations

With the “graduation” ceiling shattered by Intuit Enterprise Suite, FIXE has set aggressive targets. With continued 50% year-over-year growth and Intuit Enterprise Suite helping acquire and retain its largest, fastest-growing clients, Handel believes FIXE could get close to serving 10,000 restaurant locations in a matter of years. Looking ahead, FIXE plans to deepen its integration with Intuit, particularly by leveraging Intuit’s AI features to automate bank feeds and profitability reviews.
