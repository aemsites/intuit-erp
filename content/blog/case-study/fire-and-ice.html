Feeding first responders in the hardest places to reach

When first responders rushed to the scene following Hurricanes Helene and Milton, they waded through the aftermath of deadly storms to help victims pick up the pieces of their lives. When forest fires ravaged the Western United States, first responders were there too. Fire and Ice was on the scene as well, feeding the crews who do the hazardous work of cleaning up after natural disasters. Fire and Ice operates mobile kitchens to provide food service at scale in places it’s hardest to get, including remote parts of Alaska, forest firefighters in the Western US, and correctional facilities.

Sonia Sorce joined the mission-driven company as Controller in 2016 after years of running her own accounting firm. With first responders and disaster cleanup crews depending on it, Fire and Ice needs a business solution that keeps it running — for that, the company turns to Intuit Enterprise Suite.

I was called into servicing our community—that type of work really inspires me. It gives you purpose when you wake up in the morning.

Sonia Sorce, CFO, Fire and Ice

A new company structure leads to new challenges

Since Sorce joined in 2016, the company has grown by acquiring businesses in the mobile catering space. In 2022, under her leadership, the business transitioned to an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP) and created a holding company to bring it all together, called Fire and Ice; Sorce became CFO shortly after. Despite the company’s growth and several million in revenue per year, she maintains a lean team of three to support finance and accounting.

For many years, QuickBooks Online was a great fit. But with the heightened reporting requirements of an ESOP with multiple entities, its usual efficiencies weren’t as effective. To comply with ESOP regulations, Fire and Ice must provide consolidated financial statements to its bank quarterly and annually to ensure an accurate valuation of the business — a process Sorce describes as a “mad scramble,” adding, “It was very tedious to pull financials from so many different files.”

If we didn’t have the tools that we have in place, I’d probably have double the staff, but I like to keep things lean and utilize the tools as much as possible.

Sonia Sorce, CFO, Fire and Ice

Intuit Enterprise Suite: the right-size solution

While QuickBooks Online did many things well, the organization had become too complex for the software once it had multiple entities and ESOP reporting needs. Fire and Ice’s account manager at Intuit knew their challenges and reached out to tell Sorce about a new offering built to manage the complexities of a growing, multi-entity business: Intuit Enterprise Suite. To make the upgrade even better, all of the company’s third-party connections from QuickBooks Online Advanced carried over with no additional setup.

It’s been a positive experience having a relationship with Intuit. A one-on-one relationship is really what we need, and it’s hard to find that anymore.

Sonia Sorce, CFO, Fire and Ice

On time, on budget, and on mission

With Intuit Enterprise Suite, Sorce is able to create even more efficiencies. Intercompany journal entries, automated elimination, and clean consolidated reporting make closing the books far simpler. After using the Suite for a couple of months, Sorce also noticed she has more time to support her employees, and expects it will continue to save her time as she uses more of its features.

Now, when I need to pull consolidated reports, I can do in five minutes what I used to do in an hour.

Sonia Sorce, CFO, Fire and Ice
