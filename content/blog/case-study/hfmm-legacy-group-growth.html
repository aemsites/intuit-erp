Scaling toward $50M through acquisition

Two years ago, Jason Corby was working for a construction company when it was acquired by a private equity firm. That firm wasn’t a fit, so he left to start his own private equity firm with a few of his peers. Eighteen months later, HFMM Legacy Group is planning fivefold growth for its portfolio of outdoor service companies with a reputation of quality service in the field. The business has grown to include eight entities in two states (Pennsylvania and Florida), with over 100 employees. Despite its rapid expansion and increased complexity, HFMM has been able to reduce its tech stack and reduce time spent on manual business processes thanks to Intuit Enterprise Suite.

I’d like to be at $50 million [revenue] in five years. Right now we’re around $10 million, so I think it’s pretty attainable.

Jason Corby, Founder and CFO, HFMM Legacy Group

ERP-level capability in literally 2 hours

Intuit Enterprise Suite consolidates multiple entities in one file, providing best-in-class financial management, payment, payroll, and marketing capabilities as a full-service platform. The all-in-one solution offers enterprise-level capabilities and automation, but — unlike with a traditional ERP — migrating from QuickBooks didn’t disrupt HFMM’s business at all. Data from all of HFMM’s eight entities now flows directly into Corby’s Intuit Enterprise Suite dashboard, giving him the visibility to provide timely service to both customers and employees.

All I had to do is email my account manager, and literally two hours later, all of [HFMM’s entities’] financials and historical information were under the Intuit Enterprise Suite platform. That saved us a ton of work and made the acquisition much more profitable because of the speed of integration.

Jason Corby, Founder and CFO, HFMM Legacy Group

Top- and bottom-line growth running on one platform

Intuit Enterprise Suite quickly became the full-stack tech solution for HFMM, even with different bookkeepers in different locations and multiple entities. “Starting in the field, we use QuickBooks Time to have all our employees track time. That translates seamlessly to our finance and accounting team when they run payroll in QuickBooks Payroll. We’re using QuickBooks Bill Pay to be able to pay our vendors on time and get better visibility of when our bills are due. Our sales and marketing team uses the Mailchimp integration to share customer databases,” Corby says. Because the platform made payroll and compliance so simple across entities under common ownership, HFMM was also able to roll out a 401(k) benefit for employees that it wouldn’t otherwise have taken on.

One of the main benefits of Intuit Enterprise Suite is the amount of time it’s freed up for me. I’ve saved 10 to 15 hours a week. I can now use that time how I choose, whether it’s with family, or with more staff, or other projects.

Jason Corby, Founder and CFO, HFMM Legacy Group

Our partnership and relationship with Intuit since using Intuit Enterprise Suite has really given us unlimited support and a lot of confidence. Even if there’s issues here and there, there’s a trust that they are going to make it right… It’s a whole team of people that really want us to be successful.
