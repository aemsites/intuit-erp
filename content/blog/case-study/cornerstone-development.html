The pursuit of financial clarity for five distinct entities

When President Kristin and CEO Chad Shaules founded Cornerstone Development Company in 2017, they were supporting a vision of a changed construction industry. What started as a husband-and-wife team flipping houses has matured into a multi-entity powerhouse. The firm has now expanded to more than 70 full-time employees. Its five entities manage eight-figure construction projects, engineering for the nation’s largest public utilities, and environmental compliance management for state and federal governments.

As the company scaled from a single corporation into five distinct entities, the manual burden of financial management became a bottleneck. By migrating to Intuit Enterprise Suite, Cornerstone replaced fragmented systems and prohibitively expensive manual processes with a unified platform.

The pitfalls of manual multi-entity management

For Cornerstone, the growth from one company to five — spanning construction, consulting, and utility engineering — created an accounting nightmare. Per Shaules, each subsidiary has “drastically different needs,” requiring three distinct types of GAAP-compliant accounting within the same industry. The leadership team estimated they were spending 20+ hours a month on manual data integration in order to see a complete picture of the enterprise.

You should always drive your business like you drive your car: you spend most of your time looking out the windshield and only a little bit of time looking out the rearview mirror. Intuit Enterprise Suite is the only software that allows us to do both in an instant.

Chad Shaules, CEO and Founder, Cornerstone Development

A search for strategic foundation

Cornerstone needed a platform that could grow faster than the company itself. Cornerstone leveraged several key ERP capabilities to stabilize their foundation:

  • Multi-entity consolidation — standardize five entities with a shared chart of accounts and automated intercompany transactions
  • AI-driven insights — utilizing AI plus human intelligence to flag projects where profit margins were below expectations
  • Deep integration stack — unifying QuickBooks Payroll, Time, and Bill Pay to ensure payroll moves seamlessly from a mobile clock-in to employees’ bank accounts

Intuit Enterprise Suite gives you the foundation to understand your business. Without it, you’re flying blind, you’re haphazard, and you’re just hoping that some luck will catch you.

Chad Shaules, CEO and Founder, Cornerstone Development

By integrating Intuit Enterprise Suite with QuickBooks Time and Payroll, Cornerstone processes six-figure payrolls every Friday with a “seamless link” where employees are paid by the next day.
