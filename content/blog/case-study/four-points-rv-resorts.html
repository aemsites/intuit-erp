Expanding resort locations across the US

Four Points RV Resorts is a rapidly scaling hospitality and real estate development firm that grew from a single location in 2019 to seven locations and 14 entities across the US. According to CMO/CTO Matt Van Der Molen, as the company grew toward $11 million in annual revenue, leadership found themselves “building the rocket ship while flying it.” With their original QuickBooks Online setup, leadership was hindered by a manual back-and-forth workflow of intercompany transfers and a reporting process that took up to two weeks to produce usable documents.

By moving to Intuit Enterprise Suite, Four Points eliminated over $100,000 in fractional headcount costs, reduced reporting time to two minutes, and established a “copy and paste” model for aggressive future expansion.

Managing 14+ entities with manual spreadsheets

For Van Der Molen, the main obstacle to growth wasn’t market demand, but hitting the limits of QuickBooks Online and being bogged down “by navigating a thousand and one spreadsheets.” As the company expanded to 14 entities, the lack of a unified system meant intercompany transactions had to be handled with a higher level of expertise. The team spent up to ten hours just on chart of accounts cleanup, and even after hiring external help, spent “25 percent of the time looking at data and 75 percent teaching consultants the industry,” Van Der Molen says. Internally, the data was so fragmented that a consolidated report often ran 30 pages long because nothing matched across entities. With a summer peak staff of 250-300 people, the corporate team of 12 was drowning in manual entry, processing hundreds of invoices weekly on a system they had clearly outgrown.

It added layers of complexity that were a massive time suck.

Matt Van Der Molen, CMO/CTO, Four Points RV Resorts

The clear choice over expensive ERPs

Four Points initially evaluated traditional ERP vendors, including NetSuite, and had begun looking at Sage. Demos revealed “worrying deficiencies,” particularly in ease of use, and the cost of these vendors was “multiple times more than our existing spend,” says Van Der Molen — on top of a 90-plus-day implementation. After connecting with the Intuit Enterprise Suite team, Van Der Molen calls the moment a “no-brainer” once he realized the Suite could provide multi-entity consolidation, a shared chart of accounts, and automated intercompany allocations, all without the steep learning curve of a new platform. While competitors quoted months of transition, Intuit Enterprise Suite was active immediately.

They guaranteed it would be there when I logged in, and it was. There was no learning curve, no crazy price tag, just additional features… it was the first time a software implementation actually lived up to what was sold.

Matt Van Der Molen, CMO/CTO, Four Points RV Resorts

CFO-level optics in two minutes

Since implementing Intuit Enterprise Suite, Four Points has seen significant improvement in three areas. By automating 100% of corporate bill pay and using intercompany allocations, the team saved 20 hours per week on routine tasks — one intercompany project that previously required two hours a week was reduced to 30 minutes, a 400% efficiency gain, allowing the company to part ways with its fractional headcount and save $100,000 annually. Reporting latency dropped just as sharply: what once took two weeks — manually aggregating and cleaning up mismatched data across 14 separate entities — now takes two minutes, giving Van Der Molen real-time visibility to act on trends, like ordering more golf carts the same afternoon he spots high profit at one park. Finally, the team leveraged Intuit Enterprise Suite’s AI capabilities for vendor cleanup and chart of accounts matching to de-duplicate thousands of entries across their 14 entities, reducing fraud and abuse and freeing the corporate team from “just pushing buttons” to focus on higher-level tasks.

Aggressive revenue targets supported by Intuit Enterprise Suite

With Intuit Enterprise Suite, Four Points RV Resorts is targeting 80%+ revenue growth from $11 million to $20 million by using its “copy and paste” model for rapid acquisition. To manage this, the company plans to integrate real estate development tracking directly within Intuit’s Project Management AI, aiming to replace repetitive status calls with project milestones and capital expenditures.

Knowing we have a software team that allows us to grow gives me confidence. If you’re familiar with QuickBooks, Intuit Enterprise Suite is the upgrade you need if you’re multi-entity.

Matt Van Der Molen, CMO/CTO, Four Points RV Resorts
