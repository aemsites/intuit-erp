Transforming language services with AI

Based in Austin, Texas, Lango is a mission-driven, AI-enabled language services leader that has rapidly scaled through the acquisition of seven businesses across the US. However, this growth created a labyrinth of fragmented data that threatened to stall operations. By transitioning to Intuit Enterprise Suite, Lango replaced a manual reporting nightmare with a robust consolidated financial platform, slashing aged accounts receivable from 22% to under 2%. Today, leadership and finance teams have the real-time visibility required to manage board expectations and complex debt covenants.

The risks of raising capital with manual data

For Lango CEO and Founder Josh Daneshforooz, rapid expansion via M&A was a double-edged sword. Each of the seven acquisitions brought its own QuickBooks instance, creating a “labyrinth of logins and a maze of manual mapping” in Excel. The accounting team was losing 25-30% of its weekly capacity just trying to reconcile seven different spreadsheets for manual calculations, and because data was siloed, month-end close often dragged on for 45 days.

Without unified data, you can’t manage cash, you can’t manage growth, and you definitely can’t manage trust.

Josh Daneshforooz, CEO and Founder, Lango

Modern versus traditional ERP solutions

When Lango reached its breaking point, the traditional path was a migration to NetSuite. But the logic didn’t hold up under CFO-level scrutiny — NetSuite carried a hefty implementation fee and a recurring annual cost tens of thousands more than Lango was willing to spend, and the system felt “old-school and clunky,” lacking malleability. Lango chose Intuit Enterprise Suite for ease of use, cost-efficiency, and a consolidated multi-entity architecture, where new acquisitions could be “bolted on” simply by upgrading their QuickBooks Online account into the Intuit Enterprise Suite account.

Efficiency, velocity, and strategy at scale

Multi-entity Consolidation and Custom Financial Roles transformed Lango’s back office from an Excel center into a strategic asset. With easy access to aged AR data across all brands, Lango reduced its 90-day-plus outstanding balances from 22% to less than 2%, easing the strain on cash flow. Automated consolidation of P&Ls and balance sheets saved the accounting team 25-30% of its time, and Lango moved from a 45-day close to closing on the 15th of the month — a 66% acceleration that keeps the board informed with timely insights. By tracking billable utilization rates and cost of goods sold at the entity level, Lango also identified that its Arizona unit had low gross margins due to high contractor costs, insight that let it shift to a full-time-employee model and significantly increase profitability.

Simplicity at an enterprise level

With the financial foundation stabilized, Lango’s playbook for the future is built on simplicity. The company is targeting further acquisitions and plans to use Intuit Enterprise Suite to fold them into the consolidated financials instantly, leveraging automation and AI to streamline the finance team as transaction volume scales — positioning Lango for continued growth and a potential public offering down the road.

I want a software platform that facilitates simplification. Intuit Enterprise Suite allows us to make everything as simple as possible, but not simpler.

Josh Daneshforooz, CEO and Founder, Lango
