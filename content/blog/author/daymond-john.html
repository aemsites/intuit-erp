Daymond John

An entrepreneur in every sense of the word, Daymond John has come a long way from taking out a $100,000 mortgage on his mother’s house and moving his operation into the basement. John is CEO and Founder of FUBU, a much-celebrated global lifestyle brand with over $6 billion in product sales and a pioneer in the fashion industry. John is a celebrated entrepreneur having received over 35 awards throughout his career, including Marketer of the Year, New York Entrepreneur of the Year, was named #2 on Top Voices list for US Influencers, and has won various awards for his event, Black Entrepreneur’s Day. Beyond entrepreneurship, John shares his expertise to guide other business leaders to grow their businesses into multi-million dollar companies.
