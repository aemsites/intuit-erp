Mike Michalowicz

Mike Michalowicz is an entrepreneur, speaker, and author of bestsellers like Profit First, The Pumpkin Plan, and Fix This Next. He’s on a mission to simplify entrepreneurship, helping business owners grow by doing fewer things remarkably well. With his unique blend of humor, insight, and real-world experience, Mike has become a trusted guide for entrepreneurs worldwide. His work focuses on making entrepreneurship more sustainable, profitable, and fun. Mike’s practical, actionable advice has helped countless business owners transform their companies and their lives.
