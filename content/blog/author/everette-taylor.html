Everette Taylor

Everette Taylor is CEO of Kickstarter, the world’s premier crowdfunding platform for creative projects. Taylor also currently serves as a Member of the Board of Directors for 1stDibs, the leading online marketplace for extraordinary design. Prior to joining Kickstarter, Everette most recently served as the CMO of Artsy and also is the founder of ET Enterprises which includes brands such as MiliSense, PopSocial, ArtX and GrowthHackers.
