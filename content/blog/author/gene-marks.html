Gene Marks

Gene Marks is an author, columnist, and CPA.Gene has written 5 books on business management, specifically geared towards small and medium sized companies.

Gene now writes regularly for The Hill, The Washington Times, The Philadelphia Inquirer, The Guardian, Forbes, and Entrepreneur Magazine.

Gene owns and operates the Marks Group PC, a highly successful firm that provides technology and consulting services to small and medium sized businesses.
