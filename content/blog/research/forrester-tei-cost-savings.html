About this research

This Forrester New Technology Total Economic Impact™ (TEI) study was commissioned by Intuit to independently quantify the impact of Intuit Enterprise Suite on complex, growing businesses. Forrester interviewed 8 Intuit Enterprise Suite customers using the platform in pilot or beta, and surveyed 150 Intuit QuickBooks Online customers, to build a composite organization representing a mid-market business with 10 entities, $12 million in annual revenue, and 23 employees, including a three-person finance team. Figures in this study reflect mid-range projections for that composite organization over a three-year period.
