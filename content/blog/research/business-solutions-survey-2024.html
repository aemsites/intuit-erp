1. Business solutions for success

More than 9 in 10 (95%) respondents agree that integration between a business’s various apps and software programs is essential for growth. Eight in 10 (82%) are prioritizing steady or fast expansion over the next year.

2. Business solutions overload

Respondents’ businesses use 10 different digital business solutions on average, costing $10,000 a month ($120,000 a year) — with an estimated $3,000 a month ($36,000 a year) overspent on apps they never or rarely use.

3. A need for automation

Businesses spend 25 hours a week on manual data entry and reconciliation. More than 8 in 10 respondents say this has undermined productivity (91%), employee morale (88%), timely financial reporting (87%), and profitability and growth (85%).

4. Economic effects

Almost all (95%) respondents say rising inflation has challenged their business, and 90% say higher interest rates have made accessing financing harder. Nearly 9 in 10 (89%) agree AI is key to business success in today’s economic climate.

About this research

Intuit QuickBooks commissioned an online survey in August 2024 of 630 owners and executives from larger small businesses with 10-99 employees throughout the US. The majority of respondents (75%) are Millennials aged 28 to 43, 11% are Gen Z aged 18-27, and the remaining 14% are over the age of 44. More than half (56%) are business owners, and 2 in 5 (44%) work full-time as a CEO/President, CFO/Controller, COO/Operations Manager, or CTO/Technology Manager. Responses were collected using Pollfish audience pools and partner networks with double opt-ins, random device engagement sampling, and post-stratification based on census data to ensure accurate targeting and results.
