About this research

The 2026 Intuit Enterprise Technology Benchmark Report is based on an Intuit-commissioned online survey conducted in November 2025, which gathered insights from 2,000 senior US business leaders and executives at companies with over $2.5 million in annual revenue. The robust sample focused on finance and technology leaders (53%), with a 41-question survey tested for statistical significance at a 95% confidence level to confidently highlight differences across business cohorts.

What percentage of business leaders are consolidating technology in 2026?

Do business leaders get faster ROI from AI than from other technology investments?

Yes. According to the 2026 Intuit Enterprise Technology Benchmark Report, 80% of senior US business and finance leaders say AI delivers faster ROI than any other technology investment.

What percentage of senior leaders expect to outgrow their current technology within 12 months?

According to the 2026 Intuit Enterprise Technology Benchmark Report, 62% of senior US business and finance leaders expect to outgrow their current technology within the next 12 months.

How many business leaders are redesigning processes around AI in 2026?

According to the 2026 Intuit Enterprise Technology Benchmark Report, 92% of senior US business and finance leaders are redesigning core business processes around AI right now.

Who was surveyed for the 2026 Enterprise Technology Benchmark Report?

The report is based on an Intuit-commissioned online survey conducted in November 2025.
