Frequently asked questions

How does Intuit Enterprise Suite handle construction accounting?

Intuit Enterprise Suite gives you the power of a traditional ERP plus a more intuitive interface, a simpler onboarding process, and dedicated support—so you get exactly what your construction business needs without anything unnecessary. To learn more about how Intuit Enterprise Suite stacks up against traditional ERPs, click here. If you’re upgrading from QuickBooks Online, your construction business can benefit from the enhanced functionality of Intuit Enterprise Suite—including multi-entity management, dimensional reporting, project dashboards, and more. Manage accounting, payroll, time tracking, multiple companies, and individual construction projects from one central platform.

What can I expect while using Intuit Enterprise Suite construction in open beta?

Intuit Enterprise Suite customers in the construction industry receive automatic access to open beta capabilities—no sign-up or action required. Features, functionality, and availability may change or be discontinued as the platform evolves. While currently available to eligible customers at no additional cost, Intuit may introduce future pricing or change terms as these capabilities move toward full release. Participation remains subject to Intuit’s applicable terms and conditions.

How can I manage and monitor my business across all companies and their respective projects?

Intuit Enterprise Suite helps you deliver on budget with cross-portfolio visibility from a single dashboard. You can monitor your entire business alongside all your projects, as well as overall profitability using reports and integrations purpose-built for construction businesses. Run reports ready-made for construction, or choose from 15 new reports. You can also save time by managing multiple users, entities, locations, and subsidiaries from a single sign-in, plus, create intercompany transactions with automatic eliminations and see consolidated reports across your business.

Do I still need third-party apps with Intuit Enterprise Suite?

With features like intercompany transacting and consolidated reporting, Intuit Enterprise Suite may reduce the need for add-on tools like Reach, Fathom, LivePlan, and Syft. However, Intuit Enterprise Suite supports advanced integrations so you can continue to use apps that support industry-specific needs, such as Knowify, BuilderTrend, and HouzzPro. Never miss a beat and stay productive by integrating your current tools into Intuit Enterprise Suite.
