How intercompany eliminations work in Intuit Enterprise Suite

When entities within your organization transact with one another, Intuit Enterprise Suite automatically flags and eliminates those intercompany transactions during consolidation, so revenue, expenses, and balances aren't double-counted at the parent level.

This runs on every close cycle without manual journal entries, giving finance teams an audit-ready consolidated view across every entity in the group.
