Frequently asked questions

Is Intuit Enterprise Suite an ERP?

Yes, Intuit Enterprise Suite is an AI-native ERP designed for growing companies.

Key characteristics:

  • Modern ERP alternative—a unified platform that goes beyond basic accounting.
  • Simple and scalable—easy to use and built to scale.
  • AI-driven insights—automated experiences and intelligent business insights.
What size company is Intuit Enterprise Suite built for?

Intuit Enterprise Suite is built for businesses with growing financial complexity - especially those managing multiple entities, locations, or projects.

Is this a cloud based version of QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise?

No. Intuit Enterprise Suite is a new category of software distinct from our QuickBooks Online and Desktop products.
