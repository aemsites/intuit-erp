Frequently asked questions

What is an ERP system?

An ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) system is a comprehensive software solution for mid-to-large-size businesses. It integrates and manages key functions like marketing, accounting, sales, costing, strategic planning, and product delivery. As companies grow, leaders often evaluate their software and consider the benefits of adopting an ERP system to streamline operations and enhance efficiency.

How easy is it to migrate to Intuit Enterprise Suite?

Whether you’re migrating from QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, or a non-Intuit solution, we’re here to make the transition smooth, accurate, and effective.

Upgrading from QuickBooks Online

If you are upgrading from QuickBooks Online, your data is automatically migrated to Intuit Enterprise Suite and no additional work is needed. However, our Professional Services team will be happy to assist you if you’d like to customize the migration. Additionally, your Customer Success Manager will reach out to schedule onboarding calls and make sure your team is set up for success.

Switching from QuickBooks Desktop or a non-Intuit solution

If you are currently using QuickBooks Desktop or a non-Intuit solution, our Professional Services team can help you migrate your data to Intuit Enterprise Suite and configure it based on your needs. We can also provide information about employee training and development resources so your team can start using Intuit Enterprise Suite as soon as your migration is complete.

  • First, let your Account Manager know you are interested in migration services. Your Account Manager will arrange a meeting for you with our Data Migration team, who will scope the work and provide an estimate.
  • After you approve the estimate, provide some basic information, and give us access to your data, our Data Migration team will do the rest.
  • Our team will walk you through the migration process and timeline, provide recommendations, keep you informed along the way, and complete the migration for you.
  • Once your migration is complete, your Customer Success Manager will begin the onboarding process and you can start using Intuit Enterprise Suite. Your Customer Success Manager can also assist you with 3rd party app integrations, workflows, and reports.
