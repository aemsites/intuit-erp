Frequently asked questions

How long will it take to migrate my data to Intuit Enterprise Suite?

The migration time varies based on your current solution:

From QuickBooks Online: You’ll experience zero downtime during the upgrade. Your data is ready the moment you log back in, and your customer success manager will be standing by to help you configure your new features so you can get started immediately.

From QuickBooks Desktop: The timeline for your migration depends on your goals: some businesses want years of history, while others prefer to start fresh with a clean Chart of Accounts. Most Desktop migrations are completed in under a week. We’ll start with a data assessment to see what you need, then provide a firm estimate so you can plan your go-live date with confidence.

From non-Intuit solutions: We recognize that moving from another platform isn't one-size-fits-all. You’ll work with our professional services team to determine exactly what data is essential for your new environment. Our goal is to co-create a migration strategy that supports your current workflow and preserves your critical history—all while staying within your budget.

Will I need to relearn the interface when switching from QuickBooks Online to Intuit Enterprise Suite?
If you’re familiar with QuickBooks Online, you’re already ahead of the curve. Intuit Enterprise Suite shares the same intuitive interface, meaning your team won't need to relearn core workflows. You’ll find the same navigation and settings you're used to, but with powerful, ERP-level capabilities seamlessly integrated into that familiar environment.
What should I expect when I migrate to Intuit Enterprise Suite?

We will be your dedicated partners throughout the entire migration process, no matter what solution you’re coming from. You will be assigned a customer success manager who will stay alongside you at each phase. Together, you’ll co-create a migration strategy that supports your current workflow and preserves your critical history—all while staying within your budget.

If you need any customizations or additional solutions in your migration and implementation process, your account manager can assist you.

What data migration option makes sense for my business when moving to Intuit Enterprise Suite?

Clean slate: Start fresh, especially if your QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, or third-party tool isn’t set up correctly or you want to declutter.

Bring over lists and balances: Bring over the framework, but not all of the details. This is a great option for companies that want to make some tweaks or declutter before importing into Intuit Enterprise Suite.

Use our data conversion tool: Bring over the framework and most of the details.

What access is needed for Intuit during the implementation phase?
Before implementation starts, the professional services team will meet with you to discuss what access you’ll need to provide so that we can support you, your team, and your business goals throughout the process.
Who should I include from my team to support implementation?

A full team is not required to migrate your business to Intuit Enterprise Suite, and we will support your migration regardless of your team’s size. However, depending on the unique structure of your business and size of your team, it may be appropriate for some or all of the following to participate in the migration and onboarding process:

  • Executive sponsorship: Provides directional guidance and assesses risks
  • Project manager: Oversees all the moving parts while keeping teams on a timeline
  • Core cross-functional team: Understands the business process, can speak to any internal issues, and are able to champion this process best. This team may include power users, IT, and finance.
  • Implementation partner: Develops a rollout plan, including employee training and development, to drive product usage
What implementation path/plan should I consider?
We handle every implementation individually and can customize the process to suit the unique needs of your business. Our professional services team can individually advise you on the best way to handle your migration so that it’s smooth, seamless, and effective.
