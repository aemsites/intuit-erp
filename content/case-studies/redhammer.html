Largest US construction-focused accounting firm

RedHammer is the largest construction-focused Client Accounting Services firm (CAS) in the US, with 80+ construction accountants managing over 135 total entities. As clients scale toward the $10M–$30M revenue mark, they historically faced a challenge Partner Scott Franchini calls the “technological lull,” a point where customers outgrew basic accounting tools but were repelled by the $100,000 to $250,000 implementation costs of traditional ERPs. By partnering with Intuit Enterprise Suite, RedHammer is now migrating its 104-client portfolio into a modern, AI-powered ecosystem that bridges the gap between mid-market functionality and enterprise-grade technology.

The client fear of modernization

For RedHammer’s clients, staying on legacy systems was a massive financial and efficiency drain. Franchini states that as clients sought more functionality, they often moved to hosted solutions where “functionality goes up, but technology goes down.” These systems lacked the necessary APIs, forcing accountants to manually download and import transactions for bank reconciliations.

When onboarding new clients, RedHammer frequently encountered 4- to 8-week delays driven by heavy data rework. The root cause was rarely the software itself; instead, it was that the system had never been configured correctly, degrading data quality over time. “The idea that QuickBooks is just training wheels is one of the biggest misconceptions,” Franchini says. “The system can do far more than most companies realize. The problem is setup, not software.”

We want our clients to stay with Intuit because it’s a fully integrated ecosystem. With the functionality of Intuit Enterprise Suite, there’s zero reason for a firm to look elsewhere.

Scott Franchini, Partner, RedHammer

A construction-specific financial solution

Intuit Enterprise Suite emerged as the top choice for RedHammer by offering a modern SaaS alternative to the “archaic remote access” of desktop environments such as QuickBooks Desktop. For Franchini, the Intuit ERP specifically addressed the construction industry’s most painful hurdles through several key pillars:

  • Unified project lifecycle tracking — job costing tied directly to estimates and change orders instead of manual spreadsheet workarounds
  • Automated bank reconciliation — API-connected transactions eliminate the manual download-and-import process that used to consume hours per entity
  • Change order management — cost changes flow through the system automatically instead of requiring manual rework during client onboarding

The elimination of manual bottlenecks

The transition to Intuit Enterprise Suite has enabled RedHammer to be a true proactive solution partner for their clients. The implementation of the Intuit ERP has resulted in:

  • Accelerated onboarding — new clients configured correctly from day one, removing the 4- to 8-week data-rework delays
  • Reduced manual entry — automated bank feeds and reconciliation across 100+ entities
  • Enhanced reporting visibility — real-time dashboards replacing manual spreadsheet consolidation
  • Audit-ready controls — every transaction tracked and reconciled automatically

Intuit Enterprise Suite is the solution that finally kills what we call the ‘technological lull.’ You get the sophistication of a high-end ERP with the best-in-class user interface, all for a very low total cost of ownership.

Scott Franchini, Partner, RedHammer

Becoming a true solution provider for the construction industry

With Intuit Enterprise Suite, RedHammer no longer views the $10M–$30M revenue bracket as a point of client churn. The firm is currently moving its 38 QuickBooks Desktop clients to Intuit Enterprise Suite, viewing it as a game changer for data integrity. As Intuit Enterprise Suite continues to roll out AI-powered forecasting and externalized APIs, RedHammer plans to integrate non-financial data, such as subcontractor compliance and lien waiver status, into custom objects to create the industry’s first truly holistic construction dashboard. “We’re living through a defining moment in technology,” Franchini concludes. “Intuit Enterprise Suite doesn’t just help our clients keep up — it ensures they don’t get run over by the train of progress.”
