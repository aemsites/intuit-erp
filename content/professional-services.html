Frequently asked questions

How is Intuit Enterprise Suite better for my professional services business?
Intuit Enterprise Suite gives you the power of a traditional ERP plus a more intuitive interface, a simpler onboarding process, and dedicated support—so you get exactly what your business needs without anything unnecessary. To learn more about how Intuit Enterprise Suite stacks up against traditional ERPs, click here. If you're upgrading from QuickBooks Online, your business can benefit from the enhanced functionality of Intuit Enterprise Suite—including multi-entity management, dimensional reporting, project dashboards, and more. Manage accounting, payroll, time tracking, multiple companies, and individual projects from one central platform.
How can I manage and monitor my business across all companies and their respective projects?
Intuit Enterprise Suite helps you deliver on budget with cross-portfolio visibility from a single dashboard. You can monitor your entire business alongside all your projects, as well as overall profitability using reports and integrations purpose-built for project-based businesses. Run reports ready-made for professional services, or choose from 15 new reports. You can also save time by managing multiple users, entities, locations, and subsidiaries from a single sign-in, plus create intercompany transactions with automatic eliminations and see consolidated reports across your business.
How do I leverage the data provided in Intuit Enterprise Suite to improve decision-making?
Multi-dimensional reporting lets you streamline key decisions. Use standard reports, or leverage real-time insights to create investor-ready reports with up to 20 dimensions. You can also consolidate and categorize data across payments and accounting by line item and easily populate comprehensive reports. AI-powered recommendations based on your business' project income and cost data give you deeper project profitability insights, and drill down using industry-tailored reports, KPIs, and third-party integrations with top industry apps like BigTime, Clio, and Projectworks.
Can Intuit Enterprise Suite help me forecast future financial needs and performance?
Yes. Your last five years of performance data are used to establish baselines so you can plan ahead with accurate projections. You can also set project goals and manage project profitability with real-time job costing, profitability monitoring, and detailed reporting with custom fields. And, as regulations change, you can stay compliant and plan for the future with AI-generated forecasts and granular insights.
Do I still need third-party apps with Intuit Enterprise Suite?
With features like intercompany transacting and consolidated reporting, Intuit Enterprise Suite is built so that certain apps like Reach, Fathom, LivePlan, and Syft are no longer necessary. However, Intuit Enterprise Suite supports advanced integrations so you can continue to use apps that support industry-specific needs, such as BigTime, Clio, and Projectworks.
What is the process for migrating to Intuit Enterprise Suite?
Each migration is unique depending on your current platform and your business. Whether you're migrating from QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, or a non-Intuit solution, we're here to make the transition smooth, accurate, and effective, and we will be your dedicated partners throughout the entire migration process. You will be assigned a Customer Success Manager who will stay alongside you at each phase. Learn more about the migration process.
