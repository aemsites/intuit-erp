## Users

## Product Purpose

Intuit Enterprise Suite is a connected ERP platform for consolidated financial reporting and inter-company eliminations. It gives finance teams real-time visibility and control across multiple entities, replacing legacy ERP systems with a modern, cloud-based solution powered by QuickBooks infrastructure.

## Positioning

Enterprise-grade finance built to scale. Positioned as the modern alternative to legacy ERP systems, emphasizing speed of deployment (90% set up in under 30 days), real-time consolidated reporting, and AI-powered automation through Intuit's Finance Agent. Differentiates on migration simplicity ("set a go-live date, actually hit it") and the breadth of the Intuit ecosystem (TurboTax, Credit Karma, QuickBooks, Mailchimp).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Multi-entity consolidated reporting and inter-company eliminations
- ERP accounting powered by QuickBooks with multi-dimensional reporting
- AI agents (Finance Agent) for automated bookkeeping, forecasting, and budgeting
- Custom roles and permissions for data security
- Dedicated customer success management and guided setup
- Industry-specific workflows (construction, nonprofit, field services, manufacturing, franchises, professional services, financial services)
- Migration support from any prior software
- Constraint: enterprise-only product, not self-serve SaaS; requires a scheduled call to begin

## Product Principles

1. **Consolidation over fragmentation** — one view across all entities replaces scattered reports
2. **Speed of value** — rapid deployment and migration, measured in days not months
3. **Intelligence built in** — AI agents handle routine work so teams focus on decisions
4. **Trust through transparency** — every entry tracked, every action auditable
5. **Partnership, not just software** — dedicated success management included in the suite
