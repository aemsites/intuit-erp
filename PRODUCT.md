# PRODUCT — Intuit Enterprise Suite (erp.intuit.com)

> Descriptive snapshot of the current site, for a same-design (replica)
> migration to AEM Edge Delivery. Not a redesign brief — the target IS the
> captured current state.

## Register

`brand` — marketing / product-marketing site for an enterprise software
product. Landing + solution + pricing + comparison pages driving a
"Schedule a call" conversion.

## Product purpose

Intuit Enterprise Suite is positioned as a connected, AI-native ERP for
mid-market and multi-entity businesses: consolidated reporting,
inter-company eliminations, multi-dimensional accounting, workforce
automation, and dedicated migration support. The site's job is to
communicate enterprise credibility while contrasting favorably (cost,
time-to-value) against traditional ERPs (NetSuite, Sage Intacct, Microsoft
Dynamics Business Central).

## Users

Finance leaders (CFOs, controllers), accountants/ProAdvisors, and
operations leaders at growing mid-market companies — especially those on
QuickBooks Online/Desktop evaluating an ERP step-up, and firms in
construction, professional services, and financial services.

## Brand personality

Confident, precise, enterprise-grade but approachable. Copy is outcome-led
and quantified ("Close in days, not months", "90% set up in less than 30
days", "299% ROI"). Visual tone: clean white canvas, deep Intuit blue hero
bands, generous whitespace, product-screenshot proof, and a single
high-contrast navy primary CTA.

## Anti-references

Cluttered legacy-ERP marketing; dense feature tables without narrative;
loud/gradient-heavy SaaS aesthetics. The site deliberately reads calm and
institutional.

## Design principles (observed)

1. White-first canvas; color used deliberately (blue hero bands, sand/tofu
   neutral section fills, green as data-positive accent).
2. One primary action repeated site-wide: **Schedule a call** (navy
   `#00254a`, 4px radius). Secondary actions are outlined/ghost.
3. Large, light-weight headings (Avenir Next 400) over quantified body copy.
4. Proof through product UI screenshots, customer stats, and testimonials.
5. Consistent global header (transparent over hero) and a deep multi-column
   footer shared across every page.
