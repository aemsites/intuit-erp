Frequently asked questions

How easy is it to migrate to Intuit Enterprise Suite?

Migration is guided by a dedicated onboarding team. Most customers complete implementation in less than 2 months, and your chart of accounts, lists, and historical data come with you so your team can get up and running quickly.

Upgrading from QuickBooks Online

If you already use QuickBooks Online, upgrading to Intuit Enterprise Suite keeps the familiar experience your team knows while adding multi-entity management, advanced reporting, and automation. Your existing data transfers over so there’s no need to start from scratch.

Switching from QuickBooks Desktop or a non-Intuit solution

Coming from QuickBooks Desktop or another platform such as NetSuite or Sage Intacct? Our migration specialists help you map and move your data, validate accuracy, and confirm Intuit Enterprise Suite is the right fit before you go live.
