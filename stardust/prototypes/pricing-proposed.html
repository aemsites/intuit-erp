Why Intuit Enterprise Suite?

Getting started is easy

The migration from your current ERP takes days—not months—and the Intuit team will be there every step of the way. Guided onboarding on day one will get you off to a strong start, and your dedicated Customer Success Manager will be on call after to ensure it's smooth sailing from there.

Intuit AI supports the move

With AI automation, the platform cleans and standardizes your financial data—aligning every entity, unifying the Chart of Accounts, and optimizing shared lists for vendors, dimensions, and more.

Connect to what matters

The platform includes native workforce and money management solutions, so there's no need for additional systems. But if it's flexibility you want, you can connect seamlessly to an open ecosystem of best-in-class applications—like the ones you already use.
