Is Intuit Enterprise Suite an ERP?

The short answer, the details, and the questions finance leaders ask most before they switch.

THE SHORT ANSWER

Yes, an AI-native ERP for growing companies

Intuit Enterprise Suite is a unified platform that goes beyond basic accounting, consolidating finances, payroll, payments, workforce, and marketing into one connected system, with AI-driven insights and robust multi-entity management.

Frequently asked questions

What size company is it built for?
Intuit Enterprise Suite is built for businesses with growing financial complexity, especially those managing multiple entities, locations, or projects, that have outgrown QuickBooks Online but are not ready for a traditional ERP.
Is this a cloud version of QuickBooks Desktop?
No. It is a new category of software distinct from QuickBooks Online and Desktop, built for businesses that have outgrown basic accounting and need to scale operations.
How easy is it to migrate?
Whether you migrate from QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, or a non-Intuit solution, a Professional Services team scopes and moves your data and a Customer Success Manager runs guided onboarding.
Is support available?
Yes. Core product support is available Monday through Friday, 6 AM to 6 PM PT, and a dedicated Customer Success Manager is assigned to your account during onboarding.
